Tutorials

For ProceduralFX Studio 0.25 and later

Each one takes about ten minutes, and starts from a fresh copy of the app.

1. Your first effect in a gameStart from a ready-made fire, change it, and play it in Bevy, Godot or Unity. Includes particle systems, smooth playback, lighting, glow, pixel art and live updates. 2. Particles with a POP NetworkBuild a spark fountain from nodes: sources, gravity, wind and colour, the way Houdini does it. 3. Assets: ready-made, and your ownUse the built-in Sparks, Smoke Puff and Debris, then package your own network as a reusable node with its own sliders. 4. Vertex animation for 3D gamesBake cloth, destruction and liquids into vertex animation textures that play on the GPU in Bevy, Godot and Unity.

The basics

  • Adding nodes: press Tab in the network editor and type a node's name, or right-click.
  • Wiring: drag from a node's output (bottom) to another's input (top). Click an input to disconnect it.
  • Going inside: double-click a network node (a geometry object, POP Network or Subnet) to go inside it. Press U to come back up.
  • What you see: double-click any other node to make it the one shown (its display flag).
  • Playing: Space plays and pauses the timeline.
