Assets: ready-made, and your own

Tutorial 3 · about 10 minutes

An asset is a whole network packaged as one node, with a few sliders for what matters, like Houdini's digital assets. The app comes with four. You can make your own and reuse them in any scene, or share them with someone else.

1. Use a built-in asset

  1. Go inside a geometry object (double-click it) and add a sphere.
  2. Press Tab and look under Assets. You'll find Sparks, Spark Burst, Smoke Puff and Debris. Add Sparks.
  3. Wire the sphere into it, and click Set Display at the bottom of its parameters to show it. Double-clicking an asset goes inside it instead.
  4. Press Space. The asset's sliders (Birth rate, Speed, Spread, Life, Gravity, Drag) are its parameters, and you can key them like any other.

Sparks, Spark Burst and Smoke Puff work from a sphere or even a single point. Debris scatters boxes over a surface, so give it a shape like a grid or a sphere.

Double-click an asset to see how it's built. Inside Sparks is a POP Network like the one in tutorial 2.

2. Package your own network

  1. In a geometry object, add a subnet (Tab, then type it). Double-click it to go inside. It starts with Input_1 wired to OUT.
  2. Build your network between them. For example, add a xform (Transform) between Input_1 and OUT. Input_1 is whatever is wired into the Subnet's first input. The Subnet's output is OUT.
  3. Press U to go back up, wire something into the Subnet, and click Set Display.

3. Give it sliders

With the Subnet selected, find Promoted parameters in its parameters:

  1. In the first menu, choose a node inside, such as your Transform.
  2. In the second, choose one of its parameters, such as ty (move up and down).
  3. Type a label, such as Height, and click Promote.

The Subnet now has a Height slider, and it drives the Transform inside. Promote as many as you like. The ✕ beside each one removes it.

4. Save it as an asset

  1. Under Asset, give it a Name and a short description.
  2. Click Save as Asset. It's saved to your assets folder, and Show the assets folder opens it.
  3. Press Tab in any geometry network: your asset is under Assets, ready to place.

5. Improve it later

Change the network inside a copy, then click Save as version 2. Copies in your scenes that are still on the old version say "The library has version 2", with an Update button. Updating keeps the values their sliders were set to. Copies you don't update stay as they were.

You can also save a built-in asset again to make your own version of it. Yours then appears in its place.

Sharing assets

An asset is one .pfxasset file in your assets folder. Send it to someone, and they put it in their assets folder. It's in their Tab menu the next time they start the app, or straight away if they click ⟳ under Asset in any Subnet's parameters. Scenes that use an asset store a copy of it, so they open fine on computers that don't have it.
