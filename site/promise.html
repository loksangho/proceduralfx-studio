Our promise to you

Last updated 29 October 2026

ProceduralFX Studio should only do well when the people using it do well. These promises cover everyone who uses it, free or paid. They hold for the version you have, whatever we change later.

1. Your work is yours

We claim no rights over anything you make with ProceduralFX Studio: your scenes, renders, sprites and exports, including what you make with the AI assistant.

2. Your files stay open

Scenes are saved as plain text in a documented format, so you can read and convert them without our software. Everything you export uses standard formats, such as PNG, EXR, OBJ, USD and VDB.

3. Your license keeps working

If you pay for a commercial license and later stop paying, you keep the right to use the last version you had, forever.

4. The terms you get are the terms you keep

Each version comes with its license. We will never change the license of a version you already have. If our terms change, the change only applies to later versions, and you choose whether to move to them.

5. We never train on your work

Your scenes, prompts and outputs are never used to train any AI model. Our credits server passes your requests to Anthropic and doesn't keep them. Anthropic doesn't train on them under its commercial terms. See the privacy policy.

6. Prices are always visible

  • Before you build anything with the AI assistant, it shows what a typical request costs with the model you chose. When the request finishes, it shows what it cost.
  • Prices are in real money (Australian dollars), not points or tokens.
  • Credit never expires. Unused plan credit rolls over to the next month, and if you cancel, what's left stays yours.
  • Requests that fail with an error aren't charged.
  • The assistant is optional. You can also use your own API key or a local model, and every feature outside the assistant works without it.

7. If we shut down, the tool lives on

The source code is public today. If the project ends, it also becomes open source under the Apache License 2.0, which allows any use, including commercial use. That happens on the day we announce the project has ended, or after 18 months in a row with no new version and no commercial licenses for sale, whichever comes first. This is written into the license itself, so it doesn't depend on us keeping our word.

The license, in short

  • Free for noncommercial use, forever. Learning, personal projects, hobby games, research, and schools, charities and other noncommercial organizations.
  • Commercial use needs a commercial license. That includes effects for a game you sell. Email support@build3dstudio.com. When paid licenses have a published price, it will be on this site before anyone is asked to pay it.

The full terms are the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, plus the open-source permission described above. Both are in the LICENSE file.

Questions

If you think we've broken one of these promises, email support@build3dstudio.com.
