What's inside

A full procedural toolkit

Build geometry from networks of nodes you can change at any time. Every step stays editable, from the first grid to the final render. Familiar if you've used Houdini.

◆

Modelling nodes and VEX

Polygon modelling, booleans, scatter, copy to points, extrude, fracture, metaballs and more, plus VEX wrangles and expressions like $FF in any parameter.

✺

Simulation

Pyro, FLIP, RBD with the Bullet solver, fracturing and SOP solvers, with a flipbook to preview the result.

☁

Volumes and OpenVDB

Create, edit and render volumes, with OpenVDB support.

▦

A USD stage

Lay out shots in a USD stage context with lights, cameras and materials, and render through Hydra.

◐

Rendering with MoonRay

Production rendering with DreamWorks' open-source MoonRay renderer, built in.

↻

Animation and exchange

Keyframes with a graph editor. Import and export Alembic, USD, OBJ and FBX.

AI assistant

Describe it. Get a node network.

Type what you want, or a change to the scene you have. The assistant builds real, editable nodes, the same ones you'd place by hand.

  1. It works on a copy. Your scene isn't touched until you accept the result.
  2. It checks its own work. It cooks the nodes it made, reads the point counts and errors, looks at the viewport, and fixes what's wrong.
  3. You decide. Accept the result, and undo it in one step if you change your mind, or discard it.
